# 🎬 VibeIntake: 3-Minute Shark Tank Pitch Script

**Objective:** Record this script while screen-sharing your browser. Make sure you have your VibeIntake project running locally (`npm run dev`) and have the `DemoFiles` folder ready on your desktop to pull up during the video. The tone should be confident and fast-paced. 

---

## ⏱️ [0:00 - 0:30] The Hook & The Problem
*(Start Video. Screen should be on the VibeIntake Landing Page)*

**You:** "Hi judges. Every single day, organizations from global enterprises to local non-profits are bleeding time and data because of legacy paper processes. Paper forms are slow. Handwriting is impossible to read. And worse, they are a massive security risk. When you have Social Security Numbers written on a clipboard being passed around an office, you have a problem.

Standard OCR tools just read text—they don't understand context, they don't natively secure PII, and they don't give you a usable application at the end of the day.

That’s why we built **VibeIntake: The Paper-to-Digital Action Engine.**"

## ⏱️ [0:30 - 1:15] The Enterprise Demo & The Safety Shield
*(Click to the "Upload" page. Scroll down to the "Option 3: Quick Try" buttons or manually upload the AFL-CIO document).*

**You:** "Let me show you how it works. Let's say I'm an HR manager handling enterprise referrals. I literally just take a photo of this dense, handwritten AFL-CIO registration form and drag it into our platform."

*(Click the "Enterprise Data" demo preset button or click upload and select the AFL-CIO image).*

**You:** "Instantly, our Vision AI goes to work. But here is where we separate from the pack. Our AI doesn't just read the data; it acts as a **Privacy Shield**. It instantly recognizes that a Social Security Number and Date of Birth have been detected, and it automatically quarantines that PII. Look at the screen—the data is masked by default right on the new digital web-form."

## ⏱️ [1:15 - 1:45] The Command Center & Review Queue
*(Click 'Continue and Create Account'. Enter fake email, pass the magic link simulation, and land on the Dashboard).*

**You:** "When I land in my Command Center, I have complete telemetry over all my active digital forms. But remember the problem with AI? What if it reads messy handwriting wrong? 

Look on the left: we built a **Human Review Queue**. If our AI confidence score drops below 80% due to terrible handwriting, it quarantines the intake log so a human can safely review it without interrupting the automated pipeline."

## ⏱️ [1:45 - 2:20] Deployment & Integrations
*(Show the 'Deployed Forms' section and 'Integrations' panel on the right side of the dashboard).*

**You:** "And connecting this data is effortless. Right from this dashboard, these digitized forms can be instantly deployed to a custom subdomain—like `mycompany.vibeintake.com`—meaning you have a live, public-facing web form replacing that clipboard in seconds. Plus, on the right, you can see our live hooks directly into internal Postgres databases and webhooks for seamless data integration."

## ⏱️ [2:20 - 2:50] The "Grandma" Use-Case (The Mic Drop) 🎤
*(Click the 'New Form' button to go back to the Upload page. Click the generic 'Personal Use' demo preset).*

If I scan this into VibeIntake, our AI doesn't just parse text—it extracts temporal logic. It reads 'Sunday Dinner' and 'Dr. Smith', builds a digital timeline, and—watch this—generates a dynamic, downloadable `.ics` calendar file right here in the browser. I just turned a piece of scrap paper into actionable Apple or Google calendar events with one click."

**You:** "VibeIntake connects the physical world to digital security securely, instantly, and beautifully. Thank you for your time."
